Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. We collect as little as we can to run the arcade.

If you play as a guest

We don't create an account or set tracking cookies. Your free tokens and local high scores are saved in your own browser (local storage) and never leave your device.

If you sign in

  • Email address (and your name and picture if you sign in with Google or GitHub): to sign you in and contact you about your account or purchases.
  • Display name: shown on public leaderboards. You choose it and can change it.
  • Token balance, games played and scores: to run your wallet and the leaderboards. Scores and display names are public.

If you buy tokens

Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive a record of the purchase (pack, amount, date and Stripe's reference) and never see your card details. Stripe's handling of your payment information is described in Stripe's privacy policy.

If you contact us or report a bug

Messages sent through the contact page are stored so we can reply. Bug reports open a public GitHub issue, so don't include personal information in them.

Who processes data for us

  • Supabase: sign-in, accounts, wallets, scores and messages (servers in the United States).
  • Stripe: payments.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website; like any web host it may log IP addresses for security.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. Purchase records are kept as long as needed for tax and accounting. Contact messages are deleted once they're no longer needed.

Your choices

You can change your display name in your account menu, sign out at any time, and ask us to export or delete your account and data through the contact page. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct or delete your information; we honor those requests.

Children

Quarter Alley is a general-audience site. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; children under 13 should play as guests. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

We'll update the date above when this policy changes.
