Refund Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026. Bought the wrong pack, or something went wrong? Here is how we make it right.

Unused tokens: full refund within 14 days

If you ask within 14 days of a purchase and haven't used the tokens from that pack, we'll refund the full amount. Ask through the contact page and include the email you sign in with and the purchase date (your account menu shows your purchases).

Partly used packs

Within the same 14 days, we can refund the unused part of a pack in proportion to the tokens left from it.

Problems with a purchase

If you were charged but the tokens didn't arrive, or a game failed in a way that wasted tokens, contact us. We'll either add the tokens or refund you, whatever you prefer, at any time.

How refunds are paid

  • Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
  • When a purchase is refunded, the refunded number of tokens is removed from your account automatically.

Free tokens

Free tokens have no cash value and can't be refunded.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before disputing a charge with your bank; we can usually fix it faster. Tokens from a disputed purchase are removed, and accounts with repeated abusive disputes may be closed.
