BRICKWAVE

Keep the ball in play and clear every brick to advance. Where the ball hits your paddle sets its angle: edges send it out sharp. Silver bricks take several hits and gold ones never break. Twelve walls, then they loop back faster.

  • WWide paddle for a while.
  • MMulti: two more balls.
  • LLaser: fire with click, tap or Space.
  • CCatch: the ball sticks; launch when ready.
  • SSlow the ball down.
  • +Extra paddle. Rare.

Mouse or drag to move, click or tap to launch. Keyboard: ← → or A D, Space to launch. Gamepad works. P pause, M mute. Extra paddle every 20,000.
