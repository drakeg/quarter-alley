Rascal Road

Raccoon crossing · next 5 dens

  • Get each raccoon home. Five dens wait in the hedge on the far bank. Fill them all to clear the level.
  • Traffic is deadly. Cars, tractors, bikes and trucks speed up every level.
  • You can't swim. Ride the logs and lily pads across the creek. Lily pads with a ripple are about to sink.
  • Watch the dens. A firefly in a den is worth 200 bonus points. A snake in a den is not.
  • Beat the clock. Leftover seconds become bonus points when you reach a den.

Hop with ←↑→↓ or WASD, swipe on a phone, or use a gamepad. P pause, M mute.
