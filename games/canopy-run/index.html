Canopy Run

Sixteen treasures. Sixty-four screens of jungle. Ten minutes.

Moves

←→ run, Space or ↑ jump, ↑↓ on ladders. Jump into a swinging vine to grab it; press ↓ or Space to let go. P pause, M mute.

The jungle

Rolling logs cost you points. Tar, water, fire and snakes cost you a life. Crocodile heads are safe to stand on while their jaws are shut. Tunnels below the surface skip three screens for every one you cross, but scorpions live down there.

Score

You start with 2,000 points. Treasures are worth 2,000 to 5,000 each. Find all sixteen before the clock runs out, with three lives.
