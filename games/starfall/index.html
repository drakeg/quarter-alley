Starfall

Six towns. Three batteries. One very bad night for the coast.

Aim and fire

Point and click (or tap) where you want a flare to burst. The nearest battery with flares fires. Each burst destroys any meteor that flies into it.

Keyboard and gamepad

Move the sight with arrows or WASD (or the stick). Fire from the left, middle or right battery with 1 2 3, or Space for the nearest. P pause, M mute.

Scoring

Meteors 25, comets 100, seeker shards 125, all multiplied as the waves get harder. Unused flares and standing towns pay a bonus. A lost town is rebuilt every 10,000 points.
