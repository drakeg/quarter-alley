Iron Treads

Twenty enemy tanks roll in each stage. Hold the line and keep them away from your beacon at the bottom of the map. If the beacon falls, so does the stage.

Terrain

  • Brick breaks a chunk at a time.
  • Steel stops shells until you are fully upgraded.
  • Water blocks tanks but not shells.
  • Brush hides tanks from view.

Supply drops

  • Blinking tanks drop supplies when destroyed.
  • Star: upgrade your cannon. Shield: brief protection. Clock: freeze the enemy. Charge: wreck every tank on screen. Wrench: steel walls around the beacon. Tank: extra life.

Campaign: drive with WASD or arrows, fire with Space or J.
Duel (2 players): P1 WASD + F, P2 arrows + / or Enter. First to 5 wins. Gamepads work for both. P pause, M mute.
