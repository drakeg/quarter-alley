CUBE HOP

You're Pip, a springy fuzzball on a pyramid of 28 cubes. Hop onto a cube to change its top. Turn them all to the color shown in the corner to clear the round. Hop off the edge and you fall, unless a floating disc is waiting there to carry you back to the top.

  • Red bouncers drop in and bounce down to the bottom. Don't get flattened.
  • Purple egg hatches into Coil, a spring that hops straight after you. Ride a disc at the right moment and Coil follows you off the edge: 500.
  • Green gem freezes everything for a few seconds: 100.
  • Smudge (from level 2) changes cubes back. Catch it: 300.

Arrow keys hop diagonally: ↑ up-right, ← up-left, ↓ down-left, → down-right. Or use Q E Z C or 7 9 1 3 on the number pad. Gamepad d-pad works the same. Level 2 needs two hops per cube; level 3 flips cubes back if you land on them again. P pause, M mute.
