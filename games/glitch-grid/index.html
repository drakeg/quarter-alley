Glitch Grid

You are ZIP, a cleanup drone. Collect every data bit in the grid. Four glitches are loose in the system. Grab an overcharge core and they turn blue for a few seconds; catch them then for big points.

  • HEXComes straight at you.
  • LAGAims for where you are heading.
  • FORKWorks with Hex to cut you off.
  • DRIFTChases from far away, wanders when close.

Steer with ←↑→↓ or WASD, swipe on a touch screen, or use a gamepad. P pauses. M mutes. Extra drone at 10,000 points.
