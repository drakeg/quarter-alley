ASHFALL

One player

Move WASD or arrows

Punch J · Kick K · Special L · Block I or Space

Pause Esc or P

Two players

P1: WASD · F punch · G kick · H special · T block

P2: arrows · , punch · . kick · / special · M block (or numpad 1 2 3 0)

Specials and gamepads

Special alone or toward the foe, up + special, or back + special. Each fighter’s three moves are in the move list.

Gamepads work too: X punch, A kick, B special, bumpers block.
