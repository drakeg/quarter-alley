BUNKER LINE

Glow drones are marching on the moon base in tight ranks. They step sideways, drop a row at every edge, and speed up as their numbers shrink. Your cannon fires one bolt at a time, so make it count. The four bunkers soak up fire from both sides until they crumble away.

← → or A D to move, Space to fire. Gamepad works. On phones, use the buttons under the screen. P pause, M mute. Extra cannon at 1,500. If the drones reach the ground, the base falls.
