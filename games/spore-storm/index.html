Spore Storm

You are a firefly guarding the forest floor. A crawler winds down through the glowing spores, dropping a row every time it bumps into one. Every segment you hit turns into a new spore, and the crawler splits in two.

  • CrawlerHead 100 · body 10. Splits when hit.
  • SkitterZigzags through your patch. 300–900, more the closer it is.
  • DiverA beetle that drops straight down, planting spores. Takes two hits. 200
  • MothTurns spores purple. A crawler that touches one dives straight at you. 1000
  • SporeFour hits to clear. 1

Move with ←↑→↓ or WASD, hold Space to fire. Or move the mouse over the field and hold the button. On a phone, drag anywhere and your firefly follows and fires. P pause, M mute. Extra life every 12,000.
