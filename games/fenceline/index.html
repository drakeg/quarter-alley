FENCELINE

Claim the field one fence at a time.

Fence it off

Your marker runs along the white lines. Hold a draw button and steer into the open field to lay a fence. Reach a white line again and the side without the tangle is yours.

Fast or slow

Fast fences score 1 point per cell. Slow fences move at half speed and score double. Stop moving mid-fence and a fuse burns along it toward you.

Watch out

If the roaming tangle touches your unfinished fence, you lose a life. Embers chase you along the white lines, and more join over time. Claim 75% to clear the field; every point over is a bonus.

Arrows or WASD to move. Hold Space for a fast fence, Shift or X for a slow one. Gamepad: d-pad or stick, A fast, B slow. P pause, M mute.
