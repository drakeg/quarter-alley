Marsh Shot

Three shells. Ten birds a round. One very opinionated hound.

Shoot

Click or tap a bird to fire. You get three shells per flight; run out or wait too long and the bird gets away. The hound will have something to say about that.

Make the cut

Each round flushes ten birds. Hit the number marked on the tally to move on. The cut rises and the birds get quicker. Hit all ten for a 10,000 bonus.

Three games

1 Bird and 2 Birds at a time, or Clay Shoot: discs launch from the reeds and shrink into the distance fast. Teal 500, russet 1,000, gold 1,500, more in later rounds.

Mouse or touch to aim and fire. Keyboard: arrows or WASD aim, Space fires. Gamepad: stick aims, A fires. 1 2 3 pick a game. P pause, M mute.
