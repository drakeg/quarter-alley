LIGHTLINE

Every move you make becomes a wall.

The rule

Your rider never stops, and it leaves a solid line of light behind it. Hit any line, yours or theirs, or the arena edge, and you're out. Last rider moving wins the round.

Solo

Take on CPU riders: one at first, then two, then three, faster each round. You have three lives. Score points for every cell you travel and a bonus for each round you win.

Two players

Share the keyboard: cyan uses the arrows, pink uses W A S D. Or plug in two gamepads. First to five rounds wins the match.

Solo: arrows or WASD, or swipe on the arena. 1 solo, 2 two players. P pause, M mute.
