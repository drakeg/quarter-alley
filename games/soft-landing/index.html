Soft Landing

One tank of fuel. As many touchdowns as it lasts.

Touch down gently

Land on a lit pad with both feet, nearly upright, falling slower than 2.0 m/s and drifting less than 1.5 m/s sideways. Under 1.0 m/s is a perfect landing.

Pick your pad

Wide pads are forgiving. Narrow ones pay ×2, ×3 or ×5. The camera zooms in as you get close to the surface.

Watch the tank

Fuel carries over from landing to landing. Every landing tops it up a little (a perfect one more); a crash costs a chunk. When it runs dry, the mission ends.
