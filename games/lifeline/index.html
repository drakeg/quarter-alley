LIFELINE

Ten colonists on the surface. You are the only ship up there.

Fly

←→ fly (pressing the other way turns you around), ↑↓ climb and dive, Space fire, B smart bomb, Shift hyperspace. P pause, M mute. Gamepad works too.

Protect the colonists

Snatchers grab colonists and carry them up. If one reaches the sky, it turns into a fast, angry ravager. Shoot the snatcher, then fly under the falling colonist to catch them and set them down. Lose every colonist and the surface is lost too.

Scoring

Snatcher or ravager 150 · minelayer 250 · hound 200 · catch a colonist 500 · set them down 500. Colonists still standing pay a bonus each wave. Extra ship and smart bomb every 10,000.
