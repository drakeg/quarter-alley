BACK IT IN

Three campsites. One fifth wheel. A very judgmental lawn-chair crowd.

Campground challenge
Site 1 / 3 · Points 0

Use your steering and reverse gear to park the trailer inside the glowing campsite. Press Start to insert one token.

Keyboard: ↑/↓ to drive, ←/→ to steer (WASD works too). Touch and hold the arrow buttons. A controller's left stick steers, and triggers move. Reverse is intentionally tricky.

Park the entire trailer in the lit pad, facing the guide arrow, and stop moving. Collisions and pull-throughs cost points. Three clean sites complete the run.
