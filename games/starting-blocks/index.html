Starting Blocks

Four events. Make the qualifying mark or go home.

100 m dash

Alternate the two run buttons as fast as you can. Go before the gun and it's a false start; two and you're out.

110 m hurdles

Keep running and hit jump just before each hurdle. Clip one and you lose most of your speed.

Long jump

Build speed down the runway, then press and hold jump before the board to plant your foot. The angle climbs while you hold; let go to leap. About 20 to 25 degrees goes farthest. Three tries.

Javelin

Same idea: run, press and hold before the line, and let go to throw. Aim for about 40 degrees. Three tries.

Run: ← → or A D (alternate!). Jump/throw: Space or ↑. Gamepad: X and B to run, A to jump. P pause, M mute.
