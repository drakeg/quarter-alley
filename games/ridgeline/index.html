RIDGELINE

One tank. One valley. Everything on the radar wants you gone.

Drive

W/↑ forward, S/↓ reverse, AD or ←→ turn. Space fires. P pause, M mute. Gamepad: left stick and A or right trigger.

Read the radar

Your tank sits in the middle, facing up. Red blips are tanks, yellow are missiles, white is a saucer. The sights glow red when a target is lined up. Shells are slow enough to dodge.

Targets

Tank 1,000 · Raider 3,000 · Missile 2,000 · Saucer 5,000 (it never shoots back). Extra tank at 15,000 and 100,000.
