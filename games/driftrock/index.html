DRIFTROCK

There is no brake in space. Plan your drift.

FLY

←→ or AD rotate, ↑/W thrust, Space fire, Shift or ↓ hyperspace jump (it might drop you somewhere worse). P pause, M mute.

THE FIELD

Rocks crack into smaller, faster pieces. Everything wraps around the edges, including your shots. Clear every rock to start the next wave.

SCORING

Large rock 20 · medium 50 · small 100 · big saucer 200 · small saucer 1,000 (it aims). Extra ship every 10,000.
