BURROW BASH

Pests have moved in under the vegetable patch. Grab your air pump, dig down through the soil and clear out every last one. The deeper they are, the more they're worth.

  • DigWalk into the soil to tunnel through it. Pests follow your tunnels, and if they get bored they turn into wisps and drift straight through the dirt at you.
  • PumpShoot the hose at a pest, then keep tapping (or hold) to puff it up until it pops. Stop and it shrinks back. 200 to 500 by depth.
  • Bulb grubRound, goggle-eyed and stubborn.
  • Ember beetleBreathes a short burst of fire sideways, even through soil. Popping one from the side scores double.
  • StonesDig out from under a stone and step aside. It wobbles, then drops and squashes everything in its path: 1,000 for one pest up to 10,000 for a bunch. Drop two and a vegetable sprouts for bonus points.

Arrows or WASD to dig, Space or Z to pump. Gamepads work too. P pause, M mute. The last pest of a round tries to run for the surface. Extra life at 10,000 and 40,000.
