Spring Brawl

Wind them up. Knock their block off.

Punches

Jab is quick and light. The haymaker winds up slowly and hits hard, but if it misses, your robot is left wide open. Hit an opponent mid wind-up for a counter worth half again.

Defense

Guard blocks almost everything, and a blocked haymaker rattles the attacker. Dodge leans back so punches whiff, but you can't dodge again right away. The dial on each chest shows how close that robot's head is to popping.

Controls

1 player: J jab, K haymaker, hold S guard, A dodge (arrows and Z X work too). 2 players: P1 F G S A, P2 , . ↓ →. Gamepads: X jab, Y haymaker, bumper guard, B dodge.
