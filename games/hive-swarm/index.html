HIVE SWARM

The hive is awake. Mechanical bugs pour out in looping columns, lock into formation, then peel off and dive at your fighter. Clear every bug to reach the next stage.

  • DRONEFront line. 50, or 100 while diving.
  • WASPDives in pairs and shoots. 80 / 160.
  • QUEENTakes two hits. 150 / 400, more with escorts. Sometimes drops a capture net: lose a fighter to it, then shoot that queen to rescue it and fly two ships at once.
  • BONUS STAGEEvery fourth stage the swarm only flies past. Hit as many as you can. Get all forty for 10,000.

←→ or AD to move, Space to fire, or drag on the screen and tap to fire. Gamepads work too. P pause, M mute. Extra fighter at 20,000 and every 70,000.
